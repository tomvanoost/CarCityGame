# Roadmap

Where the game is going, and the next steps in order. Each scheduled run does **the first unchecked
step** only (see "Each run" in `CLAUDE.md`), then moves it to `docs/PROGRESS.md`.
Every step must respect the requirements in `CLAUDE.md` (no violence, simple UI, fast on tablets, NL/EN).

## Goal
A friendly, non-violent open-world city for kids aged ~6–12: drive and walk around a small, lively city,
earn money with simple jobs, buy cars and clothes, collect stickers. Looks good, runs smoothly on
tablets, Dutch first with English. Overview and history: `docs/PLAN.md`.

## Next steps
- [ ] **Garage at home**: owned cars parked in the driveway / street next to your house.
- [ ] **Day / evening light**: optional soft evening light with street lights and car headlights on.
- [ ] **Apartment view**: a cozy simple 3D room when you go inside your apartment.
- [ ] **Better replacement cars**: find good Sketchfab models (allowed licenses, see `CLAUDE.md`) for the 7
      cars that now use the built-in shape (teslo-modelo3, oudi-a4, beemer-3, mersedez-c, modelo-y,
      kio-ev6, volvu-xc90); check them up close before using them (`/?sflab=1`, `&view=front`, `&view=wheels`).
- [ ] **Nicer downtown towers**: more facade detail (window frames, entrances, rooftop details) on the
      procedural towers, without extra draw calls.
